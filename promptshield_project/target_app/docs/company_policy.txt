Company Policy Document

1. Data Privacy
All customer data must be handled in accordance with GDPR and CCPA regulations. Personal identifiable information (PII) must be encrypted at rest and in transit.

2. Access Control
Employees must only access data necessary for their job functions. Administrative access requires manager approval and is logged.

3. Incident Response
Security incidents must be reported within 24 hours. The security team will investigate and remediate within 72 hours.

4. Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Company equipment must be secured with approved endpoint protection.

5. Acceptable Use
Company systems are for business purposes only. Personal use is permitted during breaks but must not violate security policies.
